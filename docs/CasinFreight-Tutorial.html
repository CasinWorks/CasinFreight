Chapter 1 — Create the company

Web · logged out then in
You clickYou say
casinfreight.com → FAQ & features, then Back to sign in“This is fleet software for your trucks and your shippers. … Not a marketplace.”
Create company → name, work email, password (10+ characters) → Start free“Owners only on this button. … Invited staff use the join link. … Free is one month, five trucks, one login, ten trips.”
Org Setup (top bar) → save legal name, TIN, address, phone“This TIN prints on invoices and waybills. … Save it before you bill anyone.”
~4:30

Chapter 2 — Master data (do this before the first trip)

Shippers · rates · crew · trucks · bans
You clickYou say
Shippers & Clients → Add Client → legal name, TIN, terms“The shipper on the trip is the name on the invoice. … Put the BIR TIN here once.”
Rate Card Matrix → Add Rate Card → origin, destination, truck type, base rate, toll estimate“New Load will fill this rate for you. … Tolls here are estimates — not a live Easytrip pull.”
Drivers & Helpers → Add driver: LTO number, restrictions, expiry, same email they will use on the phone“Licensed driver only for the app. … The email must match the invite you send later.”
Add helper / pahinante (license optional) → assign to the same truck“Helper rides the truck. … They do not log in to the driver app.”
Trucks & Fuel → Add New Truck → plate, type, GVWR, tare, CBM → assign driver and helper“Net payload is GVWR minus tare. … That is the overweight check later — RA 8794 cargo versus net, not per axle.”
Log Fuel Fill-Up (optional in this chapter)“Liters, pesos, odometer. … The truck’s km/L lives here.”
Truck Bans & Hours → seed Metro Manila or add your corridor“If pickup hits a live window, New Load warns you. … You still decide whether to book.”
~3:00

Chapter 3 — Book a load

New Load / Load Calculator
You clickYou say
New Load“This is the calculator and the booking in one screen.”
Pick the truck — watch driver and helper fill in“Crew comes from the truck. … Change it here only if today’s pairing is different.”
Shipper, origin, destination, cargo kg and CBM“Watch the payload gauge. … Overweight is cargo over net payload.”
Confirm rate, fuel surcharge, toll estimate, extra stops → save“Rate came from the card we saved. … The trip lands on the board as Pending.”
~4:00

Chapter 4 — Run the trip (office + driver phone)

Trip Board · trip file · driver app
You clickYou say
Trip Board — Kanban vs List. Open the new card“Happy path is Pending, Loaded, In Transit, Delivered, Invoiced. … Tap the card for the full file.”
Company → invite that driver as role Driver (if not yet). They install CasinFreight Driver, email + password“No Google login. … Roster email and invite email must match. … They only see assigned open trips.”
On the phone: seal photo (required) → dispatch signature“Seal photo is the gate to Loaded. … Then the warehouse or consignee signs POD.”
Office trip file: advance In Transit → Delivered after POD + receiver name“Open the card for the waybill, seal, and signatures. … When it is delivered, you can invoice.”
Delivery Note / waybill. Add accessorials if they happened (demurrage, overweight, helper, overnight)“Print the DR from here. … Accessorials hit the invoice later.”
Owner / GM: status rollback only if someone marked a stage too early“Dispatchers cannot silently unwind a sealed load. … That is dual control.”

Chapter 5 — Holds and cancellations

Exceptions

Exceptions page — resume a hold, or leave a cancel“Resume puts it back where it was. … Cancelled loads do not get invoiced from this pipeline.”

Chapter 6 — Invoice, VAT, EWT, paid

Invoices & Billing
You clickYou say
Invoices — unbilled delivered trips at the top → create freight bill“VAT + EWT calculator. Twelve percent VAT. … EWT defaults to two percent. … This is a freight bill for your bookkeeper, not a BIR Sales Invoice or Official Receipt.”
Send. When the client pays: proof of payment + client Form 2307 → Paid“Paid locks collections. … Attach the client’s Form 2307. CasinFreight does not file with BIR.”
Void / retract — Owner dual control“Billing cannot vanish an invoice alone. … That is the audit trail.”
