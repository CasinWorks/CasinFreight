1. What we sell

CasinFreight is the operating system for a PH trucking company: trips, POD photos, VAT + EWT calculator on freight bills (12% VAT / 2% EWT), fleet, fuel, and roles. You close the owner. Dispatch and drivers use it after. CasinFreight does not register, transmit, or certify invoices with BIR — this is not a Sales Invoice, Official Receipt, or CAS/PTU e-invoice.

Default product — Founding (SaaS) They use casinfreight.com. We host. They pay monthly (or yearly). This is what you sell to almost everyone.
Exception — Perpetual license ₱300,000 once. Their own Firebase project, unlimited trucks, they pay Google. Only for large fleets who want to own the system. Not cheaper hosting.

3. How you sell (in this order)

  1. Put them on Free. One truck, real trips, real POD. Do not pitch ₱300k on the first call.
  2. Upgrade to Founding at ₱899 when they need a second truck, more staff, or more than 10 trips. Line: “Lock ₱899 now. Later this goes to ₱1,599.”
  3. Add trucks at ₱150 as the fleet grows. This is how you earn more without a new close.
  4. Offer ₱300k only if they have 15+ trucks, or they refuse to keep books on shared SaaS and want their own Firebase.
Script — small fleet (1–8 trucks)
“For your size, Founding at ₱899 is the cheaper and better fit. Two trucks are included. Extra trucks are ₱150 each. You are not buying a server. You are renting the live system, and that is cheaper on purpose.”
Script — large fleet / ownership buyer
“₱300k is not cheaper hosting. Hosting on CasinFreight is cheaper. ₱300k is if you want the system in your own Google account, unlimited trucks, and you stop paying rent per truck. You pay Google yourself. Year 1 support is included. Year 2 is ₱54,000 if you still want us on call.”

7. Objections

They say You say
“Hosting on your app is cheaper, why ₱300k?” Yes. ₱899 is cheaper for small fleets. ₱300k is ownership: your Firebase, unlimited trucks, stop renting per truck. We do not pretend it is a discount.
“We’ll wait until ₱1,599.” Founding lock is ₱899 for life while they stay subscribed. Waiting means they pay ₱1,599 as a new company.
“Can we get it on our own VPS / no Google?” No at ₱300k. That is a rewrite. Owner quotes ₱1.2M+ and months. Dedicated Firebase is the ₱300k product.
“Include lifetime support in ₱300k.” Year 1 only. After that ₱54,000/year. Unpaid support: they keep the running app, we stop.
“Just give us Free forever.” Free is 1 truck, 1 login, 10 trips, 2 GB. Real fleet needs Founding.

8. Hard rules

  • You cannot promise Founding without a confirmed PayMongo (or owner-approved promo). Promo is not commissionable.
  • You cannot change trucks, storage, or ₱300k terms in writing without the owner.
  • If two agents talk to the same TIN/company, the first qualified close is paid. Split only if the owner says so in writing.
  • Churn before month 12: you stop earning on months they did not pay. No clawback on months already paid unless the payment is refunded.
  • Do not sell perpetual to a 2-truck operator. That wastes the close and undercuts SaaS.
