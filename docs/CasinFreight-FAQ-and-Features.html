For operators, owners, and dispatchers

CasinFreight is fleet software for your own trucks and your own shippers — not a public load board. The office runs dispatch, billing, and books on the web. Drivers run GPS, seals, and e-POD on the phone.

Start free for 1 month (5 trucks, 1 account, 10 trips). New paid signups through 31 December 2026 can lock Founding at ₱899/month with 5 trucks included.

Who it is for

Company types Philippine trucking and freight operators running linehaul, wingvan, closed van, chassis, and tractor units — typically Luzon / port-to-plant work.
People who log in Owner, dispatcher, loading staff, billing, fleet manager, and drivers. Helpers / pahinante are on the roster with the truck but do not drive the app.

Driver phone app — features

  • Sign in with the same email the company invited (must also be on the Driver Roster).
  • Sees only assigned open trips (not cancelled or invoiced).
  • Live GPS while the trip is open — office sees pings on the trip file.
  • Pickup and delivery GPS stamp.
  • Camera: seal photo (required), container, cargo.
  • Signatures: driver dispatch after seal; warehouse / consignee POD with receiver name.
  • Blocks mock GPS; alerts the office if GPS is off or spoofed.

The app does not force the phone Location toggle on (that would need MDM). Helpers are not driver-app users.

Philippine ops built in

  • TIN on company and clients; invoices labeled for a VAT-registered hauler.
  • 12% VAT and configurable EWT (default 2%); reconcile with BIR 2307 and deposit slips.
  • Waybills / delivery notes with seal #, gate pass, plate, driver license, weight/volume, helper name.
  • DPWH RA 8794 overweight flag when cargo exceeds net payload (GVWR − tare).
  • LTO plates and license restriction codes; PH truck types (4W–14W, wingvan, chassis, tractor).
  • Accessorials: fuel surcharge, demurrage, overweight, multi-stop, port wharfage, toll reimbursement, helper crew, overnight parking.
  • Timezone for bans and Founding deadline: Asia/Manila.

Tolls are estimates / reimbursements from your rate card — not a live Easytrip or Autosweep pull.

FAQ

What is CasinFreight in one sentence? Software for a Philippine trucking company: office dispatch/billing/ledger on the web, driver GPS and e-POD on the phone.
Is this a marketplace where shippers post loads? No. It is for one fleet and that fleet’s clients. Shippers do not get a self-serve portal.
Can we try it before paying? Yes. Free is a 1-month trial with 5 trucks, 1 login, and 10 trips. After the month, subscribe to keep dispatching.
How do we add a helper on the truck? Open Drivers & Helpers → add a crew member as Helper / pahinante. Then edit the truck and assign Driver and Helper. New Load fills both from the truck.
Does the helper use the phone app? No. Only the licensed driver invited as role Driver, with matching roster email, uses the driver app.
How does the driver log in? Invite them under Company with role Driver, put the same email on the Driver Roster, then they sign in on the CasinFreight Driver app with email and password. There is no Google login.
Can the office see the truck live? Yes, while the trip is open and the driver has GPS on. The office trip file shows pings. You can also open the point in Google Maps. There is no separate nationwide map product.
Do you pull Easytrip / Autosweep automatically? No. Tolls are rate-card estimates and reimbursements you record. The ledger has a Tolls & RFID account.
Is overweight per axle or DPWH weighbridge math? Overweight is cargo kg versus that truck’s net payload (GVWR minus tare), with RA 8794 messaging. It is not an axle-by-axle calculator.
Does billing handle VAT and EWT? Yes. 12% VAT and EWT on invoices, plus proof of payment and BIR 2307 reconcile. The general ledger is double-entry.
How many people can log in on Free? One account and one role (Owner). Subscribe to invite dispatchers, billing, and drivers.
What happens after Founding’s first year? Price becomes ₱1,599/month. You keep the 5-truck allowance. You are not moved to List (List is 2 trucks for new companies after the cutoff).
How do we pay? In Revenue & plans, choose trucks and monthly or annual, then PayMongo checkout (GCash, Maya, card, QR Ph).
Where do we start? casinfreight.com — create the company, or join with the invite link from the owner.
